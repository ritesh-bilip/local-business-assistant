Acme Plumbing Warranty Policy

All main valves installed by Acme Plumbing carry a 24-month warranty
from the date of installation. Warranty covers manufacturing defects
and workmanship issues but excludes damage caused by misuse, freezing,
or improper maintenance.

For warranty claims, contact support@acmeplumbing.com with your invoice
number and a description of the issue. Response time is within 2 business
days.

Pricing Guide:
- Standard service call: $89
- Emergency after-hours call: $189
- Main valve replacement: $450 parts + $200 labor
